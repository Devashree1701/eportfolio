About

I design the whole measurement chain, not just one link in it.

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering at Johns Hopkins (May 2027). My work keeps returning to one question: can you trust what the device just told you? Answering it has taken me from characterizing sensor silicon on a surgical generator at Medtronic, to validating blood-pump performance at Johnson & Johnson, to training signal-processing models that pull a drone's RF signature out of noise. I'm now looking toward graduate study in electrical engineering — deepening the circuits, signal processing, and embedded systems that sit under every reliable instrument.
